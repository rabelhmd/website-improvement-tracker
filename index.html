From audit to pilot

Proposed workflow for a business that approves a pilot.

  1. Verify the baseline

    Review mobile and desktop. Capture screenshots and test a key task such as tap-to-call.

  2. Draft a focused fix

    Give an agent the verified problems and agreed scope. Review its patch on staging.

  3. Compare results and sign off

    Repeat the same tests, record the before and after, and have the owner approve release.

Audit method and limitations

The 15 November snapshot scored 196 homepages. Seven attempts could not be scored. The 50 shortlisted business sites scored 3.6–6.5 out of 10, versus a 7.4 median across 179 ranked businesses. The sample leans heavily toward tourism and accommodation.

The audit used one homepage HTML request per site. Mobile rendering, screenshots, repeat performance measurements and full accessibility review are pending. A missing header or markup pattern is a review cue. No pilot rebuild or measured improvement is recorded in this snapshot.
